# Build an AI Ad Agency with Claude + Higgsfield

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/claude-higgsfield-ai-ad-agency

Research a niche, shape an offer, build a varied ad portfolio, and test a small client pipeline with Claude and Higgsfield.

## What you'll learn

- Choose an evidence-backed niche and turn it into a specific, testable offer
- Connect Claude to Higgsfield and build a portfolio whose ads serve distinct campaign roles
- Build an intake-to-delivery workflow, then test prospects without confusing observations with financial assumptions
