# Direct a cinematic AI car commercial

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-car-commercial
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 35 min
- Lessons: 11
- Price: Free
- Last updated Aug 10, 2026

Build a cinematic car commercial from reusable assets, controlled iterations, and continuity-aware editing.

## What you'll learn

- Build named product, character, prop, and location assets into stable source frames
- Diagnose failures in performance, physics, blocking, identity, and geography
- Assemble selected moments into a coherent commercial and judge the finished cut
