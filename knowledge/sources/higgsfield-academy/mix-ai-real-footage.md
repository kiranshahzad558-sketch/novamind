# Mix AI with Real Footage

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/mix-ai-real-footage
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 5 min
- Lessons: 10
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 7, 2026

Combine AI edits with real footage by removing or adding elements, changing wardrobe and backgrounds, building transitions, reframing, and upscaling.

## What you'll learn

- Set up the footage and plugin workflow for controlled AI edits
- Remove, add, or replace visual elements, clothing, and backgrounds without losing the plate
- Build transitions and additional angles, then reframe and upscale the completed edit for delivery
