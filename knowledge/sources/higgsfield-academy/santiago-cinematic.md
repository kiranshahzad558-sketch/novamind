A 33-minute director's masterclass. A football drama built shot by shot with Claude Fable 5 and Seedance 2.0 4K.

## What you'll learn

- Break a short-film story into script beats and a shot plan
- Build character and location references, then direct camera movement, performance, and dialogue across scenes
- Select and assemble shots into a coherent football drama
