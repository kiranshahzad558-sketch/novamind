# Direct AI fight scenes through controlled iteration

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/direct-ai-fight-scenes
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 16 min
- Lessons: 5
- Price: Free
- Last updated Aug 10, 2026

Build reusable action assets, diagnose failed generations, and direct scale, transformations, impacts, and cinematic coverage without losing geography or physics.

## What you'll learn

- Prepare reusable action assets, then structure prompts with named elements, spatial measurements, timed movement, and positive constraints
- Distinguish a broken instruction from a bad generation and revise only the failing variable
- Stage scale, transformations, impacts, and cinematic coverage while preserving geography and physics
