# Build a Brand's Visuals with AI

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/brand-visuals-ai
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 26 min
- Lessons: 9
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 7, 2026

Create a complete AI brand visual system, from the brand and products to try-ons, ads, social content, TV spots, and packaging.

## What you'll learn

- Define a brand concept and design the products that make it recognizable
- Create marketing stills and try-on imagery that keep the product clear
- Extend the visual system through motion ads, social assets, a TV spot, website imagery, packaging, and unboxing content
