# The 3-Step Realistic AI Ad Workflow

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/ai-ad-3-step
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 36 min
- Lessons: 16
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 7, 2026

Build a fully AI-generated commercial from scratch — lock every asset, turn a script into a connected shotlist with a Claude skill, then generate and stitch the final scenes.

## What you'll learn

- Define a commercial story and build the product, cast, locations, and prop references it needs
- Use a Claude skill to turn the script into a connected shot list
- Generate and assemble the final commercial while protecting identity, wardrobe, and spatial continuity
