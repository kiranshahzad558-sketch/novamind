# How to evaluate AI filmmaking demos

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/seedance-25-ai-filmmaking
- Level: Beginner
- Duration: 20 min
- Lessons: 11
- Price: Free
- Last updated Aug 10, 2026

Learn to evaluate AI filmmaking demos by examining camera movement, performance, continuity, and delivery constraints.

## What you'll learn

- Identify the visual evidence that proves a camera move is holding together
- Test performance and continuity in edits, choreography, and transformations
- Judge a result against delivery constraints and record the production decision it supports
