# Add AI VFX to Real Footage

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/ai-vfx-real-footage
- Level: Advanced
- Duration: 12 min
- Lessons: 11
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 7, 2026

Turn footage you shot on a normal camera into cinematic 4K VFX with Seedance 2.0 — world swaps, creatures, and full handheld showcase shots, one sentence at a time.

## What you'll learn

- Turn any real clip into a 4K VFX shot while preserving its face, motion, and camera
- Add creatures and world-swaps that hold up on handheld, moving-camera footage
- Match added VFX to your plate's light so nothing reads pasted-on
