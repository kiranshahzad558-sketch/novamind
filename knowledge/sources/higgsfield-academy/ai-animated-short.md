# Make an AI Animated Short

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/ai-animated-short
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 17 min
- Lessons: 11
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 7, 2026

Build one animated story across eight distinct visual worlds, from concept and scene design to a finished short.

## What you'll learn

- Turn one story concept into a repeatable scene plan
- Design and evaluate distinct animation worlds for the bedroom, chase, arena, rooftop, manga, clay, wedding, and ending beats
- Assemble the scenes into a complete animated short
