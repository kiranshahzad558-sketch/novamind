# Make a Cinematic Ad End-to-End

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-ad-e2e
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 46 min
- Lessons: 10
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 7, 2026

Build a cinematic AI ad from reusable assets and a prompting framework through a 12-scene story, winning beat, and pack shot.

## What you'll learn

- Build consistent visual assets with a prompting framework for subject, setting, light, camera, and constraints
- Direct a connected 12-scene story through transformations, duels, setbacks, and a comeback
- Finish the commercial with a readable victory gag and pack shot
