# Blockbuster 4K: The AI Filmmaking Pipeline

- Course page: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/blockbuster-4k
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 40 min
- Lessons: 10
- Price: Free
- Last updated Oct 16, 2026

One 40-minute tutorial — scripting, asset creation, and scene-by-scene prompt engineering — chaptered into sections you can follow along with, shot by shot.

## What you'll learn

- Plan a cinematic story as a sequence of producible beats
- Build reusable character, prop, and location assets, then turn story beats into controlled generations
- Review and assemble selected takes into a finished short
